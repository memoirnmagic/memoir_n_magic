Frequently asked questions

  
How long does a book take?
Usually about two weeks, or a week for urgent orders. Your tracking page always shows the current estimate.

  
Do I need to send photographs?
They help our illustrators capture likenesses, but a written description works too.

  
Can I approve the story before printing?
Yes. Every order has a Customer Approval step before printing begins.

  
How do I pay?
By bank transfer or UPI after you confirm your specifications. We verify each payment manually.

  
Do you deliver outside Hyderabad?
Yes. Delivery within Hyderabad is a flat fee. For other cities we quote the courier charge separately.

  
Can the price change?
If your custom specifications need extra work we'll tell you and agree it with you first.
